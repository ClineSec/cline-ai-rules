# Locale conventions

- Hebrew replies keep the right-to-left mark after the greeting: שלום‏ (hello).
- Japanese names keep their variant glyph: 葛󠄀飾, not 葛飾.
- German long words may carry a soft hyphen from the template: Benutzer­konto.
- Dates in replies use ISO 8601 (2026-10-22); currency stays in the requester's locale (12,50 €).
